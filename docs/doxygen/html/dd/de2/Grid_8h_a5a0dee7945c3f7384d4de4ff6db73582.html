void Construct_Cell ( Cell & Grid_Cell)

Fill Face_Areas / Face_Normals / Area for a physical cell.

For structured/text quads with verts [o,a,b,c], faces follow LBRT order (0=left c→o, 1=bottom o→a, 2=right a→b, 3=top b→c) with outward normals. Do not atan2-reorder vertices after Read_Grid — that breaks wall BC normals at corners.
